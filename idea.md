Here is a card game,

the set of cards has 54 cards, where 2 cards are jokers, one red and one black.

at the bigining of the game each player is given 5 cards and the goal of the game is to play all its card.

so 5 cards per player and one card on the middle of the board (revealed). and the rest of the cards are considered as the bank and stays aside.

all cards with 7 on them when played force the next player on the line to take 2 cards from the bank.
if a joker is played, the next player on the line must take 4 cards from the bank.
card with number 2 are called "Goes everywhere" and the player can slide them under the stack.

only card of the came type can be stacked on top on each other.

the "J" allow the player who plays them to pick the next type of card the player are going to play. So if the current card on the stack is Heart and a player plays J Heart he can decide that from now one only diamond are allowed or even Heart still.

if a player don't have anything to play in his hand, he can go to the bank and pick a card on the top. a player can even go to the bank even if he has card to play. so when it is your turn you can go to the bank if you want to.

When you are sent to the bank by 7 or joker, the move to the bank is your play so you don't get to play later, if more than 2 players, after you go to the bank the next player after you is the one to play.

the only why to skip going to the bank is to have another 7 or Joker, and the number of card to take to the back is cummulated and move to the next player unless he can block with another 7 or joker

Player A -> 7, player B -> 7 player C must take 4 cards from the bank (top). in the game it is automated (the 4 cards spawn to the player C).

the card without number supposely number 1, are use to prevent the next player to play or to skip him their name are stops. if there is player A, B and C and the player A use a stop, the player B will be skipped so the next player to play is the player C.

when there is no more card in the back.... the stack is turned into the bank by keeping the last played card on the ground and the others are used for the bank in a LIFO way.

so play so you must play the color on the floor.

when the game start if the card placed on the middle is is a 7 or joker, that can goes to the bottom of the bank. if it is a J, the player decide what to play, if it is a Stop card, the first player do not play and the next player is the one who plays.
